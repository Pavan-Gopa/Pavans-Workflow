# Upstream

This is a workflow-adapted derivative of
[`DietrichGebert/ponytail`](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail), pinned
in `AI_Workflow_Kit/vendor/dependencies.lock`.

The adaptation removes global persistent mode, upstream response-format rules,
and permission to reinterpret confirmed scope. It preserves the YAGNI/reuse/
stdlib/native/minimum-diff ladder and the safety boundaries.
